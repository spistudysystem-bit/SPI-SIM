Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

Welcome to SonicBuild Academy ("the App," "we," "us," or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we handle your information and secure your data when you interact with our advanced ultrasound physical simulator application on Android platforms, mobile devices, and browsers.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in absolute data privacy and sovereign computing. Our core diagnostic models operate on-device to limit data telemetry:

  • Personal Information: We do not require, collect, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, address, or physical coordinates.
  • Authentication: If you use cloud-synchronized accounts powered by Firebase, your email address and password token are stored securely via official Firebase Authentication SDKs. These tokens are used solely to associate your diagnostic scoring benchmarks and custom physical milestones with your profile.
  • Local Storage: Custom configurations (such as your localized transducer stack layer opacities, wave velocity variables, and interactive textbook questionnaire grades) are stored safely on your physical device using local state engines. We do not transmit these custom properties to external analytics brokers.

2. Third-Party Services Included

For operations and server sync, the following secure service components are integrated:

  • Firebase Services (Firestore & Auth): Used securely to maintain cross-device sync of performance metrics, user profiles, and completed achievements if you authenticate. You can refer to Google's general privacy workflows for information on Firebase data hosting.
  • Service Worker Cache (Workbox): Coordinates files on your system to enable offline workspace access. No private analytical metadata or learning workflows are fetched or updated when offline.

3. Children's Privacy

SonicBuild Academy is designed for medical students, healthcare trainees, radiologists, and credentialed sonographers. The App does not intentionally collect any private coordinates or age-restricted information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe any such coordinate has leaked to our systems, please contact our registry support terminal.

4. Security Architecture

Any account data created by you is guarded by strict firestore rules blocks that allow read/write requests solely to authenticated owner accounts. We regularly audit configuration headers and token paths. However, please remember that no structural cloud transport method can guarantee 100% electronic resilience.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You of course retain total options concerning your local profiles:

  • Delete Account Data: You may request full master account sweeps or disconnect authentication profiles by initiating settings inquiries directly at our coordination office.
  • Offline Mode: Simply launch the application without network profiles. All core textbook modules, interactive wave simulations, and hardware controls remain completely open for exploration.
