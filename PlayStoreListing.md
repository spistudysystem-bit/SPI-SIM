## 4. Monetization & Pricing Models

You can set up SonicBuild Academy in the Play Store using one of two major approaches:

### A. Paid Application (Premium Upfront)
* Recommended Price: **$4.99 - $9.99** (Targeting a highly specialized, professional medical/technical market)
* **Pros:** Cleanest user experience; zero ad libraries needed.
* **Play Console Setup:** Under *Products -> App Pricing*, switch application type to "Paid".

### B. Free-to-Try Premium Upgrade (In-App Purchases)
* **Pros:** Maximize download volume while placing advanced modules (Doppler spectral tools, test databases) behind a one-time unlock.
* **Play Console Setup:** Use Google Play Billing Library to prompt for upgrade transactions.
